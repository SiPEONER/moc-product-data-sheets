Coolant types

OGOriginal green (conventional)
DEX-COOLDomestic vehicles; orange
G05HOAT hybrid antifreeze that meets Ford and Chrysler specs (2002 to 2012), also Mercedes (2011 and older), Audi and VW
G30OAT formula with no silicates or phosphates. Meets BS 6580:2010 for European applications
G40OAT formula with silicates for Mercedes, VW and other European makes. Meets ASTM D3306, D4985 and D6210
G48Low silicate, low pH, phosphate-free OAT formula for Volvo, BMW and Mini. Meets ASTM D3306 and D4985
P-HOATPhosphated hybrid organic acid technology (Asian makes); red for Toyota, Lexus and Scion, blue for Honda and others

Important

Always follow the vehicle manufacturer's coolant specification.
