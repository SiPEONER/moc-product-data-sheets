GM 6.6L EGR and Induction Cleaning Tool

New16501

MOC has developed a new tool to clean the EGR Valve and Cooler System along with the Induction System for the 6.6L GM Diesel engines. The EGR Tool, along with Optimizer Product (MOC# 16501- sold separately), will safely assist in removing deposits in these systems, and related areas. The adapter connection location and full user instructions are included with each tool.
