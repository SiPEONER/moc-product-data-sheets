Coolant Adapters for Audi and Ford Vehicles

New94356

MOC has developed 2 new adapters to service the cooling systems in various Audi and Ford vehicles (Part Nos. 94356 and 94357). These adapters are known to fit the 2011-up Ford Fiesta, Focus, Fusion, Escape and Transit. These adapters also fit a variety of Audi vehicles. These adapters connect at the pressurized reserve tank.
