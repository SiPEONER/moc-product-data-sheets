Basic Brake Machines

Update9806098026

MOC has changed some of the internal hoses on the Basic Brake Machine (Part No. 98060) to accommodate its adjustable, higher working pressure. In addition to the hose changes, a small filter Part No. 98026 has been added to the service hose to prevent clogging of the selector valve.

What's new

An Upgrade Kit (Part No. 98759) is available with all the necessary components to upgrade you Basic Brake Machine.

If you have any of these machines in your inventory please contact the Machine and Equipment Department at (866) 244-6621 to determine if these upgrades are needed for your machines.
