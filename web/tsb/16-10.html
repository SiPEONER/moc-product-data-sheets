New Design for Smart Tech II ATF Machine

95852

MOC has updated the look for the Smart Tech® II ATF machines. The chassis is dark gray instead of black, and the face panel has the SMART TECH® II title in white text. This design change makes it easier to differentiate between the Smart Tech® I and Smart Tech® II ATF machines. For existing machines in the field, the new face panel is available separately as Part No. 95852.
