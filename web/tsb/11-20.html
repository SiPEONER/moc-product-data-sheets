Smart Tech Fluid Filling Station

New74367

MOC has a new machine available for automatically refilling spray canisters with liquid and compressed air. The spray canister is 32oz and available with numerous different nozzles and spray patterns.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

This machine allows the user to purchase products such as Brake Wash and Parts Wash in bulk, 5 gallon or 55 gallon, and use the Fluid Filling Station to fill the reusable spray canister as needed.

See the attached Ad sheet for more details and images of the machine and spray canister.

Contact
