Transmission Adapter 90° Female 3/8" Shallow Quick Disconnect

New9478894688

MOC has developed a new adapter for use on various vehicles (Part No. 94788). It is known to fit some GM, Dodge and Chrysler vehicles. This adapter is a 90 degree version of Part No. 94688.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

This new adapter can be used with either of the Male Quick Disconnect adapters (Part Nos. 94609 and 94682) to perform a Transmission Fluid Flush.

For more information, contact the Machine & Equipment Department at 866.244.6621.
