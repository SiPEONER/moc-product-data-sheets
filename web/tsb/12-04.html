Power Steering Fluid 06181 and Revised Formula 01231

New0618101231

Effective March 1, 2012, the formula for Power-Steering Service Solution #01231 will be updated, while remaining suitable for use in all power-steering systems with the exception of: (1) Honda®/Acura® vehicles; and (2) vehicles that require full-synthetic (Ultima-Green) or mineral oil. This change is a result of available additive technology in a competitive marketplace, which was discussed at the recent Distributor Conference.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

MOC will now offer three tiers for Power-Steering Fluid: (1) #01231, which can be used on the vast majority of vehicles excluding Honda®/Acura®; (2) #06181 (a new product called Tech Grade Power-Steering Service Fluid), which is the same formula as the previous #01231 and suitable for stores that service Honda®/Acura® vehicles; (3) #06121, which is for vehicle models that require full-synthetic fluid.

Kit #03771 (a 2-pack kit) will include the #06181 Tech Grade Power- Steering Service Fluid. The existing kit #2161 will contain the new formulated #01231 fluid.

Important: There will be two methods for distinguishing the old version of #01231 from the new. First, any batch code dated February 15, 2012, or later, will be the new formula. Second, for the next three months, a red sticker will be placed on bottles and cases of the new formula. We strongly recommend that you rotate your older inventory accordingly to avoid confusion. Product #06181 & #03771 will be available March 9, 2012.
