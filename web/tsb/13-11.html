90 Degree Intermediate Adapter Hose for Transmission Machines

New94855

MOC has developed a new 90 degree Intermediate Adapter Hose (Part No. 94855). This new Hose is especially needed to connect to transmission adapters in tight spaces where a straight connection will not fit.

What's new

This Hose assembly can be used with all versions of the ATF machines.
