Engine Flush Adapter for Hyundai/Kia 4.6L Engines

New96129

MOC has developed a new Engine Flush Adapter for 4.6L Engines found in various models of Hyundai and Kia vehicle's. (Part No. 96129). This adapter is known to fit the Kia Borrego, as well as the Hyundai Genesis and Equus.
