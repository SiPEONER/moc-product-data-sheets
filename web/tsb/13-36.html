Fuel Adapter Seal Replacement Kits

New7305273051

New Seal Replacement kits are available for the 3/8" (Part No. 73052) & 1/2" (Part No. 73051) Ford Fuel adapters and plugs. Each kit includes the standard adapter O-Rings (2) and Latch Spring (1).
