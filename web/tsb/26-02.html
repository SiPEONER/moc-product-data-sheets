2-Tube Coolant Adapter

New95398

A new cone adapter for the 2-Tube Coolant machine, for vehicles with shorter, wider radiator openings, such as certain Dodge models.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

Effective immediately, the adapter ships with every new 2-Tube Coolant machine, which now includes four adapters.

Key benefits

  • Seals better on difficult radiator neck designs
  • Faster, more reliable coolant exchange
  • Less chance of leaks or improper seating during the service

For more information, contact the Machine & Equipment Department at 866.244.6621.
