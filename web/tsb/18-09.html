3/8" Female Quick Disconnect 90 Degree Fuel Adapter

New73066

MOC has developed a new 3/8in. female fuel adapter. This adapter is Part No. 73066. This new 90 Degree Quick Disconnect adapter is necessary to use on some vehicles where the connection is close to the fire wall or other engine parts.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

This adapter is known to fit 2014-newer GM trucks and SUV's with the 5.3L & 6.0L engines.
