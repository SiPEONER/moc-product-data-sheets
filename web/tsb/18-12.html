Volvo Banjo Fuel Adapter

New73067

MOC has developed a new Banjo fuel adapter for various Volvo vehicles with 3.0L and 3.2L engines. This adapter is Part No. 73067. This new Banjo adapter is necessary to fit some newer vehicles where the connection to the fuel rail is close to the fire wall.

What's new

This adapter set is replacing the 73037 & 73038 Volvo quick disconnect adapters.
