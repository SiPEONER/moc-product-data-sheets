Mercedes Sprinter EGR Valve and Cooler Cleaning Tool

New7237516501

MOC has developed a new EGR Valve and Cooler Cleaning tool for the 6 cylinder and 4 cylinder Mercedes Sprinter vehicles. The EGR Tool (Part No. 72375), when used with the Optimizer Product (Part No. 16501) (sold separately), will safely assist in removing deposits in the EGR Control Valve, Cooler, and related areas.
