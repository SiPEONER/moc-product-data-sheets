Insert for Spin-On Filter Transmission Adapter

New94783

MOC has developed a new insert for use with the spin-on filter transmission adapter (Part No. 94783). This insert screws into the 94783 adapter, which is designed for use on transmissions with an external filter that has a ¾ - 20 thread.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

This insert is known to fit on the Allison Transmission external filters.

NOTE: This insert can also be used with the Oil Filter Base Adapters (Part Nos. 74740 & 74742).
