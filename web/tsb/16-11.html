How to Minimize Cross-Contamination of ATF Fluids on ATF Exchanging Machines

94195

The MOC Machine and Equipment Department offers ATF Machine New Fluid Tanks in different colors to minimize cross-contamination when using different automatic transmission fluids:

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

  1. Connect the New Fluid tank with the desired ATF fluid to the machine.
  2. Connect one of the open-hose adapters to the black service hose.
  3. Confirm that the machine is next to an ATF waste container.
  4. Hold the open-hose adapter over the ATF waste container.
  5. Using the "Top Off" feature on the machine, press and hold the top off button for 10 seconds. If the new ATF fluid is a different color, you will see it coming out of the open-hose adapter.
  6. The machine is now ready for the next service.

In addition, when changing the New Fluid Tank the MOC Machine and Equipment Department recommends purging the ATF Machine before starting a service with a different transmission fluid.
