Demo Kit for the Universal Turbo Mister Adapter

New71381

MOC's new demo kit allows you to visually see the complete atomization/mist of the Universal Turbo Misting adapter (under vacuum) while used in connection with the EDR-3 Fuel Service Machine or Module. This new kit is Part No. 71381. Demonstration procedure instructions are included with each kit.
