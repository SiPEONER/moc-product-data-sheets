Universal 20in Optimizer Tool

New75008

MOC has developed a new Optimizer™ Tool to service various models. This new Tool was designed to be universal.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

This tool is designed for vehicles where the throttle body is located below the exhaust manifold, such as Volvo and Chrysler vehicles. The Intake Air Boot is removed and the Tool is inserted. The Intake Air Boot is then pulled over the Tool and air filter/throttle body housing.

MOC Optimizer™ Product (Part No. 16501) is sold separately.
