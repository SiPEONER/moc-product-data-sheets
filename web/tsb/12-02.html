Brake Service Announcement Draining the Master Cylinder

Notice

: It has been brought to our attention that removing all the fluid from the brake fluid reservoir, while doing a brake fluid flush service, can possibly lead to air in the brake system.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

It is our recommendation that you leave at least a ½" of fluid in the reservoir when evacuating the old fluid at the beginning of the service.

Contact

For more information, contact the Machine & Equipment Department at 866.244.6621.
