EDR-3 Firmware Update v.1.9

Notice71247

An important update that must be installed in all EDR-3 machines and modules as soon as possible.

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

What's changing: when choosing an induction or GDI service, the customer is asked to enter the engine size (for example 4, 6 or 8 cylinders). The EDR-3 then uses a strategy specific to that engine size.

What you need to do: the firmware is available by email, or order a USB drive (part 71247) and ask for the firmware to be preloaded before it ships. Contact the Equipment Department.

Contact

For more information, contact the Machine & Equipment Department at 866.244.6621.
