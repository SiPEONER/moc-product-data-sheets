4 Hose Conversion Adapter for Basic Brake Machines

New98184

MOC has developed a new adapter that will convert the Single Bleeder service hose on the Basic Brake machines into 4 Bleeder service hoses (Part No. 98184). This is an external adapter that is connected to the suction hose allowing the user to use the machine as a single hose or 4 hose machine.
