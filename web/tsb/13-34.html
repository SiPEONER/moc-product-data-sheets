Cold Weather Warning Smart Tech Transmission Machine

Before beginning a transmission service, make sure all new transmission fluid is at least 40 degrees Fahrenheit. Due to the viscosity of the new transmission fluid in cold weather conditions and the extreme cold weather we are experiencing, it is possible that the fuse on the Smart Tech™ Transmission Machine may blow. The use of transmission fluid at a temperature of 40 degrees Fahrenheit and above should ensure proper working conditions of the Smart Tech™ Transmission Machine.
