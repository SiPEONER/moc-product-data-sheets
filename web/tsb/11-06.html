Deluxe Induction Tool

New7217272175

MOC has developed a new and improved Deluxe Induction Tool (Part No. 72172). The Deluxe Induction tool will be replacing the existing Induction Tool (Part No. 72175).

Download PDFView PDFAll bulletins

What's new

The improvement will have many benefits such as; adding a vacuum brake to prevent liquid from being siphoned into the intake manifold, and; potentially allowing large amounts of liquid from being ingested into the combustion chamber when the engine is restarted. In addition, having the air breather closer to the control valve allows for a better regulated application of the product during the service. It will also allow for easier inspection and replacement of the breather when needed.
